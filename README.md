# Leaving the Bike Behind

A country-by-country report on parking an Australian-registered motorcycle somewhere between Sydney and London, flying out, and coming back to it later.

**[Read the report → leaving-the-bike-behind.md](leaving-the-bike-behind.md)**

The question sounds simple. It turns out to contain three separate questions that almost every forum thread and government page conflates:

1. Can you physically and legally leave the country without the bike?
2. Does the temporary-import clock stop while you are gone?
3. Can the paperwork be renewed while you are away?

Almost everyone asks the first. The second is the one that actually governs the trip, and almost nobody asks it. In nearly every country on the route, a temporary admission period is calendar time from the moment of import. It does not care whether the bike is being ridden or sitting under a dust sheet.

The report covers 39 jurisdictions from Timor-Leste to the United Kingdom: the temporary-import document and its maximum stay, whether you can exit without the bike, whether the clock stops, named storage businesses with prices, the realistic worst-case penalty, and a difficulty rating. Every claim is tagged HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW or UNKNOWN, and Part 7 lists the twenty things the research could not establish.

Compiled July 2026 from public sources: Horizons Unlimited, ADVrider, Caravanistan, GT-Rider, Ride Asia, iOverlander, Mad or Nomad, Team-BHP, blogs and ride reports, cross-checked against customs authorities, transport ministries, EUR-Lex, gov.uk, and government travel advisories.

Two warnings carry across the whole document. The favourable evidence skews 2012 to 2019, and enforcement on this route has been tightening since 2020. And because Myanmar and the Wagah border are shut, very few people are currently riding a foreign-plated bike across this route at all, so several countries have no documented precedent either way.

Border statuses, fees and rules on this route change fast. Verify anything you plan around, and get the important things in writing from the relevant customs authority.

Corrections and newer firsthand evidence are welcome. Open an issue or a pull request.
